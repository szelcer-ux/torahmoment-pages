  22. 21:59:58Steven Goldburd[WHEN THOU NUMBEREST THEM.] The meaning of that there be no plague among them, when thou numberest them is: if they do not give atonement money at the very moment of the numbering, a plague will strike. However, Yefet says it means that if they are numbered and do not give their ransom, they will fall before their enemies when they go to war.73In other words, that there be no plague among them, when thou numberest them refers to defeat by their enemies.
