  16. אני טרם אכלה לדבר אל לבי והנה רבקה יצאת…
  17. The רמב"ן is troubled by and gives an explanation as to how אליעזר knew רבקה’s name since we don’t find anywhere that אליעזר was actually told her name. Additionally, this entire episode seems a bit strange. Why should בתואל actually believe אליעזר? Maybe אליעזר made up the entire story in order to receive בתואל’s blessing for the marriage? Furthermore, later in פסוק נ', לבן and בתואל tell אליעזר to take רבקה as a wife for יצחק because “מיהו-ה יצא הדבר” and the מדרש tells us, from where did it come - from הר המוריא. Shouldn’t it have said from אליעזר’s story about his test?
  18. When the תורה first mentioned that רבקה was born (פרק כ"ב פסוק כ"ג), Rashi explains that Hashem at that time informed אברהם that רבקה the wife destined for יצחק was born. Presumably, when אברהם told אליעזר to find a wife for יצחק, he must have told אליעזר that Hashem said יצחק’s wife’s name would be רבקה.
  19. This would explain how אליעזר actually knew רבקה’s name when he recounted his story to בת
